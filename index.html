Custom Jeopardy Game Maker

Create and share interactive trivia boards in your browser without accounts or databases—everything is saved directly in your shareable link.

Create a New Jeopardy Game View Live Example (Spanish Jeopardy)

How to Use the Jeopardy Maker

  1. Open the Creator: Launch the builder and set a custom title.
  2. Add Categories & Clues: Input your five categories, questions, and answers.
  3. Generate Your Link: Create a custom URL storing your entire board.
  4. Play or Share: Play immediately or send the link to others.
