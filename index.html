How to Use the Jeopardy Maker

  1. Open the Creator: Launch the game builder and give your trivia board a custom title.
  2. Add Categories and Clues: Fill in your five categories along with questions and answers for each point value.
  3. Generate Your Link: Click to generate a custom URL that stores your entire game board.
  4. Play or Share: Open your link to play immediately or send it to friends, classmates, or students.
